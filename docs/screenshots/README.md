For each real capture, record the capture date, application version/commit, actual site/run/case/alert ID as relevant, acquisition dates, `live` or `archive_replay` origin, and method label in its caption or accompanying notes. For model screens also retain checkpoint SHA-256 and report/split provenance. A base map is not the dated analysis input.

Keep passwords, tokens, private mailboxes and sensitive identifying information out of captures. Seeded recipients use reserved `.example` addresses. Illustrative zones have approximate boundaries and must remain labelled. Describe any suspected activity without accusing a named person/company of illegality.

Do not overwrite old evidence to make it look new. A new live scene may not exist during the demo; show the real no-new-scene state or a clearly badged completed replay instead.
